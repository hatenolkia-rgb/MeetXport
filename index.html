FAQs

Questions exporters ask us

Do you offer an instalment option for payment?

Yes. You can pay in instalments rather than one upfront amount. Tell us which service you're interested in and we'll share the instalment plan for it.

Do you work on a commission basis?

No. Meetxport isn't a commission-based agent or a typical service provider. We're export enablers: we build and run your buyer pipeline for a set fee, and the conversations and deals that come out of it stay entirely yours.

Do you use cold data to find buyers?

No. We're strictly against using cold data for buyer finding. We don't send bulk messages to bought lists. Every buyer we approach is researched and matched to your product and target market first.

Can you help with anything apart from buyer finding?

Yes. Meetxport is an ecosystem for import-export services, not just buyer finding. For example, we help with shipment insurance and claims and arrange meetings at trade events. Tell us what you need and we'll guide you to the right support.
