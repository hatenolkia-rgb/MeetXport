Privacy Policy

How Meetxport collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

1. Who we are

Meetxport helps Indian businesses find and meet international buyers and supports them with export services. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our website, fill in a form, message us, or respond to our advertisements on Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details: your name, mobile / WhatsApp number, email address and city or country.
  • Business details: company name, whether you are an importer, exporter, service provider or just starting, how long you have been in business, your products and the services you are interested in.
  • Messages: what you write to us through our website form or chat, Instagram or Facebook messages and comments, WhatsApp and email.
  • Advertising information: when you submit an Instagram or Facebook lead form, Meta shares the answers you gave together with the name of the form, campaign and ad.
  • Notes from our team: details of calls, meetings, quotes and payments related to your enquiry.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, call you and explain our services.
  • To send you messages you asked for or would reasonably expect, such as a welcome message, our WhatsApp community link, meeting details or a payment link.
  • To deliver the services you buy from us.
  • To keep records of our conversations and improve our service and advertising.

You can ask us to stop sending you messages at any time by replying "STOP" on WhatsApp or by emailing us.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Members of the Meetxport team who handle your enquiry.
  • Service providers that run our systems on our behalf, such as Meta (Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp), our website and database hosting, and our email provider.
  • International buyers, partners or service providers, but only where this is part of a service you have asked us for.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your information sooner, unless we must keep it by law.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent to being contacted, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Email admin@meetxport.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

Access to your information is limited to our team and protected by login controls. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time by publishing a new version on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact admin@meetxport.com, call +91 83196 24287, or write to Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, India - 201310 or Raipur, Chhattisgarh, India.
